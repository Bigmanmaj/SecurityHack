# Procurement Thresholds

Purchases under five thousand dollars a year need only the budget owner's approval and a completed vendor record. This is the path most software tools take, and it is intentionally light so that teams do not route around it.

Purchases between five and fifty thousand dollars a year need a second quote or a written justification for sole sourcing. Procurement does not require the cheapest option to win; it requires the reasoning to be written down at the time rather than reconstructed later.

Purchases above fifty thousand dollars a year go to a competitive process with at least three suppliers approached. The evaluation criteria are fixed before the responses are opened, and the scoring is retained with the contract for audit.

Multi-year commitments need finance approval regardless of annual value, because they move money between budget years. Auto-renewing contracts are treated as multi-year commitments unless the renewal can be cancelled with ninety days notice or less.

Renewals are not automatic approvals. A renewal reopens the vendor record, reconfirms the internal owner, and reconfirms the data classification. A renewal that arrives with under thirty days notice is escalated, because the negotiating position is already lost by then.
