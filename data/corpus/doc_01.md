# Nimbus: plans and pricing

Nimbus has three plans: Starter at $8 per user per month, Team at $15, and
Business at $29. Starter includes 100 GB of pooled storage, Team includes 1 TB,
and Business includes 5 TB plus audit logs and SSO. Annual billing is 20 percent
cheaper than monthly and is charged as a single invoice at the start of the
term. Plan changes take effect immediately, and the customer sees a prorated
adjustment on their next invoice.
