# Nimbus: support hours and escalation

Support is staffed 09:00 to 18:00 UTC on weekdays, with a first response target
of four hours on Team and one hour on Business. Anything involving data loss,
suspected account compromise, or a billing dispute over $500 is escalated to a
duty engineer immediately, at any hour. Agents escalate by tagging the
conversation `escalate-duty` and summarising what the customer has already
tried. Customers on Starter receive email support only and are not covered by a
response-time target.
