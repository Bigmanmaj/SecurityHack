# Nimbus: cancellation and data export

Cancelling a workspace stops the next renewal but leaves the workspace active
until the end of the paid period. After cancellation the data stays available in
read-only form for 30 days, and is permanently deleted on day 31. A workspace
owner can export everything as a single archive from Settings, Data, Export,
which is prepared in the background and emailed as a download link. The link
expires after seven days, and a new export can be requested at any time before
deletion.
