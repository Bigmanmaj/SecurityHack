# Travel Guidelines

Book travel as early as the trip is confirmed. Fares booked inside fourteen days cost roughly twice as much on average, and the travel desk reports that late booking is the single largest controllable line in the travel budget.

Economy class is the default for all flights under six hours. Longer flights may be booked in premium economy with manager approval. Business class is reserved for flights over ten hours and requires approval from a director, recorded on the booking.

Hotels are booked through the corporate travel desk at negotiated rates. If a conference offers a cheaper room block, book it and note the conference on the claim. Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel is fine and no incentive payment is offered for doing so.

Ground transport should be the cheapest reasonable option for the circumstances. Ride-hailing is reimbursable; so is a rental car when public transport would add more than an hour to the journey. Parking fines and speeding tickets are never reimbursable.

International travel requires a duty of care check before booking, which the travel desk runs automatically. Travel to countries on the restricted list needs security sign-off, and the list is republished on the first of every month.
