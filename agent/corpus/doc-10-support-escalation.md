# Support Escalation

Tier one support answers within one business hour during coverage windows. Tickets that cannot be resolved with the runbook are escalated to tier two with the diagnosis so far attached, because re-asking the customer for information they already gave is the most common source of complaints.

Tier two owns the ticket until resolution or escalation to engineering. Escalating to engineering requires a reproduction or a clear statement that reproduction failed and why. Tickets escalated without either are returned, and the return carries a note explaining what is missing.

Customers on the enterprise plan have a named contact and a four-hour response commitment covering severity one and two. The commitment is measured from ticket creation, not from the first human reading it, so out-of-hours coverage is staffed accordingly.

Escalation to engineering does not transfer ownership of customer communication. Support keeps the customer informed on an agreed cadence, and engineering supplies the technical substance. This is the same separation the incident process uses, for the same reason.

Recurring tickets on the same cause are grouped and raised as a product issue rather than escalated individually. The support team publishes the top five recurring causes every month, and product teams are expected to respond in writing to the top two.
