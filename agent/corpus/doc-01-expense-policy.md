# Expense Policy

Employees may incur reasonable business expenses without pre-approval up to two hundred dollars per transaction. Anything above that threshold needs written approval from a cost-centre owner before the money is spent, not after. Retroactive approvals are declined as a matter of routine.

Receipts are required for every claim regardless of amount. A photograph of a paper receipt is acceptable; a bank statement line is not, because it does not show what was purchased. Claims without an itemised receipt are returned to the claimant rather than rejected outright.

Travel bookings go through the corporate travel desk. Booking directly and claiming the cost back is permitted only when the travel desk is closed and the trip starts within twenty-four hours. In that case, attach a short note explaining the timing when you file the claim.

Client entertainment is capped at one hundred dollars per attendee per event, and the names of all attendees must be recorded on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursable within that cap. Gifts to public officials are never reimbursable under any circumstance, at any amount.

Claims must be filed within sixty days of the expense being incurred. The finance team runs a reimbursement cycle every second Tuesday, and claims approved before noon on the Monday prior are included in that cycle. Anything later waits for the following cycle.
