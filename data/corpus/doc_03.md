# Nimbus: password reset

A customer resets their password from the sign-in page by choosing "Forgot
password" and entering the email on the account. The reset link is valid for 60
minutes and can only be used once; requesting a new link invalidates the
previous one. Support agents cannot set or read passwords, and must never accept
a password from a customer over chat or email. If the reset email does not
arrive, check for a typo in the address and ask the customer to look in spam
before escalating.
