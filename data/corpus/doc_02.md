# Nimbus: billing cycles and invoices

Monthly plans are billed on the same calendar day the workspace was created, and
annual plans renew on the anniversary of that date. Invoices are emailed to the
billing contact and are also available under Settings, Billing, Invoices for the
last 24 months. Every invoice shows the plan, the seat count on the billing
date, and any prorated adjustments from mid-cycle changes. Support agents can
read a customer's plan and next billing date with the `lookup_account` tool, but
cannot edit invoices.
