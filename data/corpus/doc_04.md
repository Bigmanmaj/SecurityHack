# Nimbus: two-factor authentication and account security

Nimbus supports two-factor authentication with an authenticator app or a
hardware security key; SMS codes are not offered. When a customer enables 2FA
they are shown ten single-use recovery codes, and each code works exactly once.
A customer who has lost both their device and their recovery codes must complete
identity verification with the workspace owner before access is restored.
Support agents cannot disable 2FA on a customer's behalf.
