# Nimbus: seats and team management

Workspace owners and admins add or remove members under Settings, Members.
Adding a seat mid-cycle charges a prorated amount for the remainder of the
period, and removing a seat leaves a credit that is applied to the next invoice.
A removed member loses access immediately, but files they owned stay in the
workspace and are reassigned to the workspace owner. Only the owner can transfer
ownership, and a workspace must always have exactly one owner.
